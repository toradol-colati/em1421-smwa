AI Usage Policy 
The use of AI generative systems, such as ChatGPT or similar platforms, to conceptualize, draft, or develop either project proposals or final projects is prohibited. Proposals that show clear evidence of relying on such systems for their core ideas or structure will not be accepted. AI tools can sometimes assist with brainstorming or refining ideas; however, excessive reliance on them undermines the originality of the analysis and limits the development of critical thinking skills. This project is designed to encourage independent engagement with the subject matter, foster creativity, and promote a deeper understanding of the research process. Students are expected to base their work on their own insights, supported by course materials and independent research, to maintain the integrity and originality of their proposals.
	



1 Goal 
The goal of the project is to perform an original data analysis by applying tools, methods, and techniques addressed during the course. In particular, you are asked to: 
1. Identify a research question and motivate the need for the analysis; 
2. Support your work with appropriate references to scientific publications (minimum 5), which must be included in the bibliography; 
3. Collect relevant data to address the research question; 
4. Apply quantitative methods and techniques to analyze the data, paying particular attention to the visualization of the results: 
5. Interpret and discuss the results; 
6. Acknowledge the limitations and biases of the work (if any). 
2 Data 
1. You are free to use whatever data you want; 
2. You can combine data from different sources; 
3. You can rely on available datasets, provided that the research question underlying the analysis is original.

1. The report must not contain (a description of) the code used to generate the results; 2. The topic of the project must be previously agreed with the instructor; 
3. The entire analysis should be performed in R or Python;

The project discussion is conditional on passing the oral exam (theoretical questions on course content). For the discussion, you will have 5 minutes (7 minutes for groups of two) to present the goal and key findings of your work. Slides are optional but welcome.
